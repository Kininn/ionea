# Look, effects and sound: whole colour presets, stained glass, Reduce effects hides effects
# and shows plain colours, the sleep dim, one pop-up sound per burst, and the system list (it threw before).
# Setup: test\w4-look-sound.ps1 (12 dummies, fake emulators at <root>, --windowed --mute, off screen).
# No semicolons in comments: they split lines.
wait 900
type Tester
key accept
wait 900
key accept
wait 2200
zoom medium
folderfill Arcade|5
# 1. Colours: whole palettes only, Default first, a one-colour id saved before becomes its nearest palette
settings look and layout
key accept
cardrow Colours
expect rowvalue Colours|Default
key right
expect rowvalue Colours|Aurora
key accept
wait 500
shot w4-colours-list
key back 3
wait 700
colours blossom
wait 500
shot w4-blossom-light
colours red
wait 300
settings look and layout
key accept
cardrow Colours
expect rowvalue Colours|Sunset
key back 2
wait 600
colours ionea
# 2. glass: stained white in the light look, stained near-black in the dark
expect glass #B3F3FDFE
key left 12
expect name folder:Arcade
key accept
wait 900
shot w4-glass-light
key back
wait 700
theme dark
wait 600
expect glass #C214282E
shot w4-home-dark
key left 12
key accept
wait 900
shot w4-glass-dark
key back
wait 700
# 3. Reduce effects: the glows and running lights are hidden, the ring is one colour
expect effects shown
expect ringplain no
reduce on
wait 500
expect effects hidden
expect ringplain yes
shot w4-reduced-dark
reduce off
wait 500
expect effects shown
expect ringplain no
theme light
wait 600
# 4. sleep: the dim fades in, then out
sleep on
wait 250
shot w4-sleep-fading
wait 1000
expect asleep yes|1.0
shot w4-sleep
sleep off
wait 800
expect asleep no|0.0
# 5. pop-ups: three in a row make one sound, one after a pause makes its own
wait 4500
toastmark
toast One
toast Two
toast Three
wait 9500
expect toastsounds 1 sounded, 2 quiet
wait 4500
toast Four
wait 500
expect toastsounds 2 sounded, 2 quiet
wait 3000
# 0. the systems list of a new emulator opens and scrolls to its end (it threw before)
addemu T\custom\MyEmu.exe
wait 600
expect screen SystemPickCard
key down 40
wait 300
expect cardfocus Other systems
shot w4-systempick
quit
